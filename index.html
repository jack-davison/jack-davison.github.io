I work across the whole data workflow: scraping and cleaning messy data, building R packages and Shiny apps, writing reproducible reports, and teaching other people to do the same.

Most of that work has been in air quality and environmental analytics. At WSP (formerly Ricardo) I build reporting, tooling and training for public and private sector clients, and I’m a core maintainer of the openair family of open-source R packages. Before that I did a PhD at the University of York, building statistical models from hundreds of thousands of real-world vehicle measurements.

What I do

  • R package development I maintain {openair}, {openairmaps}, {worldmet} and {deweather}, and led the move of {openair} from lattice to ggplot2 in version 3.0. I also wrote {ukaq}, {euroaq} and {leaf.magic}, and I’ve had features merged into open source projects like {leaflet}, {dygraphs} and {prismatic}.

    R · Git & GitHub · devtools · pkgdown · CRAN releases

  • Reproducible reporting Quarto reports that turn raw data into a polished output, whether ‘dynamic’ HTML dashboards or ‘static’ Typst-powered PDFs.

    Quarto · R Markdown · Typst

  • Data visualisation Static and interactive graphics with ggplot2, plotnine, leaflet and gt. Runner-up and honourable mention in Posit Table and Data Visualisation Contests.

    R · Python

  • Shiny apps Dashboards and internal tools for clients and colleagues.

    Shiny for R · bslib

  • Teaching Posit Certified Tidyverse Instructor. I’ve designed workshops for and taught data analysis to learners from Defra, the Environment Agency, UKHSA, CASANZ, and the University of Gibraltar.

    R · tidyverse

Software

Lead developer. Combines openair’s analysis with interactive leaflet maps, most notably putting bivariate polar plots on maps for source triangulation.

{leaf.magic}
Software
