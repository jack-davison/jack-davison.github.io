Software

{openair}

The long-standing R toolkit for air quality analysis. Core maintainer; led openair 3.0, moving every plot from lattice to ggplot2.

{openairmaps}

Lead developer. Combines openair’s analysis with interactive leaflet maps, most notably putting bivariate polar plots on maps for source triangulation.

{worldmet}

Co-maintainer. Downloads and tidies global surface meteorological observations from NOAA’s meteorology databases, ready for use with openair.

{deweather}

Co-maintainer. Uses machine learning models to remove the influence of meteorology from air quality time series, revealing underlying trends.

{ukaq} and {euroaq}

Author. Lightweight R packages for accessing UK and European Environment Agency air quality data.

{leaf.magic}

Author. Builds customisable leaflet markers from modern icon libraries (Font Awesome, Bootstrap, Lucide, Ionicons) in any colour and size, with matching legends.

Reports & apps

UK AIR Local Air Quality Dashboard

A public Shiny dashboard for Defra. Enter a postcode to see local monitoring, compliance and council action plans for any UK local authority.

UK Hydrocarbons Network Annual Report 2024

A Quarto report published by Defra each year, summarising measurements of benzene and other VOCs and tracking compliance with the Air Quality Directive.

UK Summer 2023 Ozone Episode

A rapid-turnaround report on a protracted summer ozone episode, using then-new features in {gt} and {bslib}.

Air Quality on Bonfire Night 2023

Particulate pollution on Bonfire Night, and how well the Daily Air Quality Index works for short-lived events.

West London Particulate Episode 2023

An episode report with interactive leaflet maps, styled {gt} tables and {ggplot2} figures.
